About Us

Cat cafe is a place where you can grab a cup of coffee by the company of cats. Some of them was adopted from the stray cat shelter or either adopted at the street. All of them have been vaccinated with the most basic vaccines so you dont have to worry. The most important that you need to know is, you can bring your furry kids here too with only follow some basic rules to keep the safety for each other.

Suggested duration: 1-2 hours
