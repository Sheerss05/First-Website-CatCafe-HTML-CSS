Frequently Asked Questions

  • Where are you located?

    We have a physical shop which located at Tunku Abdul Rahman University of Management and Technology in Setapak area. You can walk in enjoy of food or order in our website, we ship within Malaysia.

  • When Will I Receive My Order?

    You can be sure that you will receive the freshest coffee or food possible from our cafe at Cat Cafe. We will send our orders no later than 48 hours after you have placed your order with us.

    We send all our orders with Gmail and will use a courier if the order is over 2kg.

  • Do You Deliver Overseas?

    Unfortunately due to the increasing complexity and Customs and Duty charge of crossing borders at the moment, we now only offer delivery to Malaysia until further notice.

  • You guys have free Wi-Fi, right?

    Yes, we have Wi-Fi and it is free. Members just get preferred access (it's faster).

  • What payment options do you offer?

    We accept payment by Credit Card, Debit Card & bank transfers for orders on our website.
