Our Story


It was a beautiful day, no rain, no storm. And at that beautiful moment, I met her. She called Olivia, and she was my first cat. Before I adopted her, she was a poor stray cat that already wandering for three years. After that, more and more stray cats have been adopted by me. Because of that, I decided to open a cafe accompanied by them. That's how our story began, and it started at the year 2018.
